5. Map display (OpenStreetMap / Leaflet)

The interactive world map of endangered World Heritage sites uses the Leaflet library together with map material from OpenStreetMap. The map tiles are loaded from servers of the OpenStreetMap Foundation, St John’s Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, United Kingdom, which transmits your IP address. The Leaflet library is delivered via the unpkg content delivery network (Cloudflare), which likewise transmits your IP address. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. More information: wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy.
